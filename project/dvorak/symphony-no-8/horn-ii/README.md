# Dvořák 8 / Horn II

The attached source is an F-horn part. Physical PDF pages are mapped as Movement I: pp. 9–11 (page 11 systems above the printed Adagio divider only); Movement II: page 11 below that divider plus p. 12; Movement III: p. 13; Movement IV: pp. 14–15. Page 16 begins a different part and is out of scope.

The independent GPT-6-Sol audits exposed material errors in prior transcriptions. Movement I contained accidental glyph fragments incorrectly counted as noteheads; the page-11 B heads are natural, and eight additional D5 heads lack reviewed durations. Movement III's first report assigns cue/stem positions as Horn notes and misses a bass-clef section; system 6 starts on A2. Existing HTML/PDF for Movements I and III are therefore archived under `superseded/` and must not be used for practice.

Movement II's independent audit maps all 16 systems and checks five pitch anchors, but note, cue, tie, and rhythm coverage is incomplete. Movement IV received a limited independent audit of 11 + 12 systems, treble clef, no printed key signature, and three pitch anchors; the full note/rhythm transcription remains incomplete. **Continuous playback is blocked for all four movements** pending a complete corrected note-level and rhythm audit. The raw source PDF, page images, and OMR files are not in this repository.
