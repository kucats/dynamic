# Nabucco / Sinfonia / Trombone II

This is the tenor trombone part, read in the printed bass clef. The pitch-reading deliverable covers physical pages 2–4 of the 34-page source part; it is not a complete reading of all 34 pages.

## Pitch-checked excerpt

- 401 noteheads across 38 systems were checked against the source: page 2 has 124, page 3 has 125, and page 4 has 152.
- The check covers bass-clef pitch spelling, octave, key signature, explicit and carried accidentals, and conventional basic trombone slide position.
- B-natural follows the sample's `H` notation; B-flat is written `Bb`. Sharps are retained in the spelling.
- The published A3-landscape PDF has 21 pages and keeps the original notation and printed note lengths visible, with pitch/octave and position labels alongside it.
- The note-level audit receipt is `reading-audit.json`.
- An exploratory synthesized player is available at `public/composers/giuseppe-verdi/nabucco-sinfonia/trombone-ii-p2-4-checked/exploratory-playback.html`. It asks for acknowledgement before playback or single-note audition.

The source is recorded by SHA-256 and is not included in this repository. IMSLP lists the matching 34-page Trombone 2 item (#856391) in the Ricordi/Kalmus part set as Public Domain. The supplied PDF was not byte-compared with the IMSLP file, so that listing is provenance evidence rather than proof that the two files are identical.
